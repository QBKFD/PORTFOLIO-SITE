Deep learning for commodity markets: Transformers and RNNs on gold, silver and copper

MSc thesis · reworked after submission

Whether neural networks can forecast next-day returns of gold, silver and copper. After fixing look-ahead in the original pipeline, none of 24 model and metal combinations beats "no change": the inputs predict how much prices move, not which way.

TensorFlow · PyTorch · XGBoost · Walk-forward · Diebold-Mariano · Model Confidence Set

Deposit pass-through under stress: NL and BE savings, 2022–2026

Personal project · public ECB data only

The standard deposit pricing model, fitted on data up to 2021, forecasting Dutch and Belgian savings rates through the 2022 hikes and 2024 cuts. The training window mattered more than the model, and no specification significantly beats a one-number rule.

ECM family · Dynamic forecasts · Diebold-Mariano · NII impact

XAI under pressure: what rule-based models actually cost

BSc thesis · University of Amsterdam

An independent benchmark of RuleOpt and three other rule-based methods against black-box baselines on eleven datasets. Interpretable models kept up on simple tasks and fell more than 15 points behind on complex multiclass ones.

FinBERT sentiment across 18,755 earnings call transcripts, asking which part of the call carries the signal. Prepared remarks do; the Q&A skews neutral.
